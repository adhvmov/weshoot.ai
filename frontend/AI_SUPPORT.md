## 4. FAQs & Troubleshooting

**Q: Why is my result blurry?**
A: Try using the "Improve Quality & Upscale" tool. For specific faces, choose the "People" preset which has face restoration.

**Q: I ran out of credits, what do I do?**
A: You can either upgrade your plan for a monthly allowance or purchase a one-time "Add-on" pack from the pricing page.

**Q: Can I use images for commercial purposes?**
A: Yes, you own the commercial rights to all images generated on paid plans (Essentials, Pro, Business).

**Q: What is the difference between specific and creative modes in AI Photoshoot?**
A: Precise mode sticks strictly to your text description. Creative mode allows the AI to "hallucinate" complimentary details to make the image look better, even if you didn't ask for them.

**Q: How do I get 4K downloads?**
A: 4K resolution is available only on the Pro Plan and Business Plan. Essentials and Free plans are limited to 2K.
